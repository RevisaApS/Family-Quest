## Layer 2: Indirect Competitors

### Simplified Tabletop RPGs
- Examples: Hero Kids, rpgKids, No Thank You Evil
- Price: $5.99-$29.99 one-time
- How it solves: Pre-made adventures + simplified rules for parents to DM
- **Opportunity:** Partner with or license content from these creators

### D&D Beyond + Official Tools
- Price: $0-$30/month
- How it solves: Digital character sheets, dice rollers, rules reference
- **Opportunity:** Not AI-powered, not kid-focused, not story-driven

### Parent-Led Homebrew
- Price: $0-$20/month for AI subscription
- How it solves: Using ChatGPT/Claude directly to improvise
- **Opportunity:** YOUR ORIGIN STORY - you know exactly what's missing

### DnD Adventure Club
- Price: $11.99/month subscription
- How it solves: Monthly printed adventure books for kids delivered by mail
- **Opportunity:** Proves parents pay monthly for kids D&D content

## Gap Analysis

### Table Stakes (Must Have)
- AI-generated story responses
- Character creation
- Basic inventory/stats tracking
- Mobile app (iOS + Android)
- Free tier to try

### Opportunity Gaps (No One Does Well)
- Kid-safe content moderation
- Family/multiplayer mode around ONE device
- Physical dice integration (hybrid play)
- Multi-language TTS (especially Danish/Nordic)
- Screen-light design (look-listen-discuss philosophy)
- Age-appropriate difficulty scaling
- Parent DM assist mode (not replacement)

### Common Complaints from Competitor Reviews
| Complaint | Your Solution |
|-----------|---------------|
| "AI goes off the rails / inappropriate content" | Kid-safe guardrails |
| "Too complex for beginners" | Simplified for ages 7-13 |
| "Can't play with family" | Built for family |
| "Expensive for what you get" | Fair freemium |
| "No voice / TTS support" | Quality TTS core feature |
