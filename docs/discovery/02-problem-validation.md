## Frequency Assessment

**Estimate:** Weekly to bi-weekly

**Triggers:**
- Weekend family time
- Kids see D&D in media (Stranger Things effect)
- Friends at school talk about it
- Parents want quality bonding time

**Evidence:** Multiple sources confirm 1-2x/week as realistic; sessions often end early due to kid attention spans.

## Nordic Market Signals

### Screen Time Concern: HIGH

Nordic countries are actively legislating against excessive screen time:

- Sweden pulling back on "phone-based childhood" (After Babel, Nov 2025)
- Denmark's "Copenhagen Test" for raising screen-smart kids
- Nordic countries "tackling the scourge of screens" (The Local, Feb 2025)

### Opportunity

The "screen-light" positioning aligns perfectly with Nordic parenting values. Danish language support creates barrier AND opportunity—solving it = defensible moat.

---

## Strongest Signal

Dedicated communities (TTRPGkids.com with 400+ articles, Reddit threads, ENnie Awards) prove ongoing parent demand. AI storytelling apps for kids exist and monetize—but **none combine D&D + family + screen-light design**.

---

## Biggest Concern

The "AI DM" space is getting crowded fast (AI Dungeon, Friends & Fables, Enchanted RPG). Differentiation MUST be the family/kids + screen-light + multi-language angle, not just "AI does D&D."
