## Top-Down Analysis

### Total Addressable Market (TAM): $2.5-3.0B

| Segment | Size | Notes |
|---------|------|-------|
| TTRPG Market | $1.9-2.0B | 2024, growing 11-12% CAGR |
| Kids Education Apps | $6.3B | 2024 |
| AI Storytelling Apps | Emerging | Fast growth |
| **Overlap Segment** | $200-400M | Family/kids RPG + AI storytelling |

### Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM): $50-100M

**Reasoning:**
- English-speaking families with kids 7-13: ~30M households (US, UK, Canada, Australia)
- Nordic/European families: ~10M households
- Assume 10% interested in D&D-style family gaming = 4M households
- Assume 25% would consider an app solution = 1M households
- At $10/month average = $120M theoretical annual market
- Discount for competition/alternatives = $50-100M realistic SAM

|-----------|---------|-------------------|
| Year 1 | $50K-100K | 500-1,000 |
| Year 3 | $500K-1M | 4,000-8,000 |
