# Phase 1: Problem Brief

## The Idea

**Problem:** Parents want to play D&D with young kids but lack the DM skills/prep time, while existing digital games create isolated screen time rather than family interaction.

**Who has it:** Parents with children aged 7-13 seeking quality family bonding through imaginative play.

**Current state:** Either struggling with complex D&D rules, using fully digital games (too much screen), or improvising without structure.

**Proposed solution:** LLM-assisted DM app with image generation, text-to-speech, simplified mechanics, and physical dice integration—designed for look-listen-discuss interaction rather than constant screen viewing.

## Key Features Envisioned

- Character/story setup
- Inventory management
- Battle mechanics
- Dice selection (user's physical dice)
- Image generation per turn
- Text-to-speech narration (Danish critical)
- Screen-light design philosophy

---

## Core Philosophy

> "In today's world, kids are viewing screens isolated, which is what we want to change. On this app, we don't have to view the screen non-stop. Look at the image, hear the story, and then interact with each other, discussing what we should do and roll the physical dice."

---

## Problem Severity Assessment

**Score: 7/10**

Reasoning: Founder has already built a workaround with Google AI Studio, indicating strong motivation. The Danish TTS failure shows even motivated users hit walls with current tools.

---

## What Must Be True

1. The problem is painful enough that parents will pay
2. Kids will actually engage with the solution
3. The "screen-light" design works in practice
4. Multi-language TTS (especially Danish) is achievable
5. AI can generate kid-appropriate content reliably
