Proposed Stats (4 Simple Stats)

Kid-friendly, encourages different play styles including non-combat.

💪 Strength

Physical power, melee combat, breaking things, carrying heavy objects

✨ Magic

Casting spells, enchantments, understanding magical things

⚡ Agility

Speed, stealth, dodging attacks, acrobatics, picking locks

❤️ Heart

Courage, kindness, making friends, talking your way out, helping others

Why "Heart"? Encourages kids to try non-combat solutions. "Can I talk to the dragon instead of fighting?" — Heart makes that viable.
