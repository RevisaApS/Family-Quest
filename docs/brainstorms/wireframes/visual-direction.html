Key Differentiators

1

Depth & Texture

Gradients, subtle shadows, warm glows instead of flat colors. Feels like candlelight, not fluorescent office.

2

Serif Typography

Georgia or similar for headers gives storybook feel. Sans-serif for body keeps readability.

3

Ornate Borders

Subtle gold/amber accents on cards. Not over-the-top medieval, but hints of craftsmanship.

4

Magical Motion

Gentle glows, particle effects on buttons, page transitions that feel like turning pages.
