Dark Mode Color Palettes

Eye-friendly backgrounds with standout accent colors

Base Principles

  • Background: Not pure black (#000) — adds blue/purple undertone for depth
  • Text: Not pure white (#fff) — slightly warm/cool tint reduces eye strain
  • System default: Follows user's OS preference, defaults to dark
  • Accent colors: Fantasy-themed, high contrast against dark backgrounds

A) Mystic Purple & Gold

Recommended
#0d0d14 Background
#1a1a2e Card/Surface
#2d2d44 Elevated
#f0a500 Primary
#9b6dff Magic/Accent
#faf8f5 Text

Vibe: Royal, magical, premium. Purple undertones in the dark give depth. Gold feels treasure-like and warm. The cream-white text is easier on the eyes than pure white. Classic fantasy palette that feels special.

Vibe: Enchanted forest, nature magic, rangers and druids. Green is calming and natural. Deep forest black is very easy on the eyes. Feels more specific to outdoor/nature fantasy than general D&D.

My Recommendation: A) Mystic Purple & Gold

Why:
• Purple undertones in the black make it feel magical and premium
• Gold is universally associated with treasure, achievement, and fantasy RPGs
• Cream-white text (#faf8f5) reduces eye strain vs pure white
• Purple magic accent (#9b6dff) gives a secondary highlight for spells/magic moments
• Feels distinctively "D&D" without being generic

Alternative: If you want something warmer and more action-oriented, B) Obsidian & Ember has a great dungeon-crawl energy.
