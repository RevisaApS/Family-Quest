Visual Styles & Difficulty

Parent-controlled settings — clear names, clear expectations

Visual Styles (3 Options)

Affects AI image generation AND narrative tone. Parent sets this once, changeable in settings.

🌈 🐰 ✨

Whimsical

Ages 5-8

Images: Bright colors, rounded shapes, friendly creatures, storybook illustrations
Story: Gentle adventures, no scary moments, problems solved with kindness and creativity

⚔️ 🐉 🏰

Realistic

Ages 7-12 Default

Images: Detailed fantasy art, dynamic action, cool-looking characters and creatures
Story: Exciting adventures with real challenges, mild tension allowed, heroes always prevail

🌑 💀 🗡️

Dark

Ages 13+

Images: Moody lighting, atmospheric scenes, mature fantasy aesthetic
Story: Complex themes, real consequences, genuinely challenging situations

Difficulty Levels

How challenging should the adventure be? Affects dice thresholds, consequences, and AI behavior.

🌱 Easy

First-timers
  • Dice: Low thresholds to succeed (roll 2+ on d6)
  • Setbacks: Minor inconveniences, never feel stuck
  • Hints: AI suggests good options more clearly
  • Pace: Shorter encounters, quicker wins

⚔️ Medium

Default
  • Dice: Fair thresholds (roll 3-4+ on d6)
  • Setbacks: Meaningful but recoverable — "plot twists"
  • Hints: Options presented equally, player chooses
  • Pace: Balanced encounters with tension and release

🔥 Hard

Experienced
  • Dice: Higher thresholds (roll 5+ on d6)
  • Setbacks: Real consequences — choices matter
  • Hints: Minimal guidance, figure it out together
  • Pace: Longer encounters, earned victories
